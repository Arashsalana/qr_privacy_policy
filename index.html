Legal & Privacy Documentation

Privacy Policy for Luminia: Block Puzzle

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Publisher: Malwa Labs Studio • Package: com.malwalabs.luminia
Privacy Summary: Malwa Labs Studio respects your privacy. Luminia operates on a local-first architecture. We do not collect names, phone numbers, contact lists, biometric data, or precise location coordinates.

1. Introduction

Malwa Labs Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Luminia: Block Puzzle (the "Game" or "App"). We are dedicated to providing a secure and privacy-focused gaming environment. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you download, install, launch, or play Luminia.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Luminia is engineered to minimize data collection. We do NOT collect, request, sell, or rent any of the following:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): Real names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • Financial Data: Credit card numbers, bank credentials, or billing details.
  • Device Hardware Sensors: Camera footage, microphone audio, photo library, or local documents.
  • Precise Location: GPS coordinates or background location tracking.
  • Address Book: Contacts, friend lists, or calendar entries.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

To preserve your high scores and unlocked themes without requiring an account or cloud login, Luminia stores non-personal game data locally on your device:

  • Gameplay Statistics: High score, all-time best score, total lines cleared, and combo streaks.
  • Game Preferences: Active visual theme, audio volumes, and haptic feedback toggles.
  • Virtual Currency: In-game Lumina Coins and Radiant Gems earned through gameplay and rewarded ads.
  • Unlocked Items: Acquired themes and achievement badges.

This data remains strictly on your local hardware and is never synced to commercial tracking databases.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To maintain Luminia as a free-to-play puzzle game, the Android application integrates the certified Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect certain technical device parameters in compliance with industry privacy standards:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used in accordance with Google Play Policy to deliver contextual or personalized ads and prevent fraud.
  • Device Information: Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Network Information: Coarse IP-based regional location to serve ads in the correct language and currency format.
  • Ad Metrics: Ad impressions, click events, and video completion events (to verify rewarded ad rewards).

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete / Reset Advertising ID.

For more details, review Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Optional Global Leaderboards (Supabase)

If you choose to participate in daily challenge leaderboards, minimal non-personal gameplay metrics are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS: your chosen nickname, score, lines cleared count, and timestamp. No personally identifiable credentials or passwords are ever required.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Luminia is designed for general audiences of all ages (rated Everyone / 3+). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Ad requests are configured in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

7. Rights Under GDPR (EEA & UK Players)

Players residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom enjoy statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access & Portability: View your local data at any time directly on your device.
  • Right to Erasure: Permanently delete all stored gameplay records using the in-game "Reset All Saved Data" button.
  • Consent Withdrawal: Revoke ad personalization at any time through Android system settings.

8. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to know whether their personal information is sold. Malwa Labs Studio does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

9. 1-Click Complete Data Wipe (User Control)

You maintain full control over all stored data at all times:

  • In-Game: Navigate to Settings → Reset All Saved Data to instantly wipe all local progress.
  • Android System: Open Settings → Apps → Luminia → Storage → Clear Data to purge all local files.
